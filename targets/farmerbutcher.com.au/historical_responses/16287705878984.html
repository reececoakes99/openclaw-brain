WM Meats

We're a family-run farm with a deep-rooted passion for delivering top-notch meat to families like yours. Our journey in farming began early on; Matt's childhood was spent assisting his grandfather with corn-cutting and cattle feeding during school breaks, while I dreamt of being on a farm since I was a little girl. Together since the age of 16, our shared love for farming has driven us to devote every spare moment to improving the farm owned by Matt's parents. With their support, we acquired our own farm where we now raise beef in the quaint town of Gladstone on the Mid-North Coast.

At WM Meats, we pride ourselves on raising beef on grass and supplementing their diet with DDG Pellets. We are committed to hormone-free, antibiotic-free, and chemical-free farming practices, ensuring the health and well-being of our animals and the quality of our products. All our pastures and grasses are nourished with 100% natural fertilizers.

Our two young children, Matilda and Thomas, play an integral role on the farm. Matilda, bold and compassionate, adores animals and lends a hand whenever she can. Thomas, full of energy and a love for the outdoors, thrives in nature.

At WM Meats, our mission is to offer sustainable, premium-quality meat at reasonable prices. We're grateful for your support in choosing us as your meat provider!

Description

Pasture-raised lamb is a wholesome choice for your meals. Unlike conventional methods, where sheep are confined in feedlots, pasture-raised lamb grazes freely on open fields, and this is good for several reasons. First, it’s healthier. The lambs enjoy a natural diet of grass and plants, which results in leaner, more nutritious meat. You can savor the authentic taste of lamb without worrying about excess fat.
