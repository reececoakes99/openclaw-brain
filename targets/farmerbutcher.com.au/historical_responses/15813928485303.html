THE PANTRY

WHAT IS THE PANTRY

We will be curating a monthly subscription box featuring locally grown produce and handmade products from across Australia. Each box will highlight different regions, showcasing Aussie-made items and helping small-scale producers share their goods with you. This initiative aims to celebrate local craftsmanship and support small businesses by bringing their unique products to your doorstep.

Before we start this phase, we need to get some local producers on board. To do this, we'll roll out a limited number of trial boxes, allowing us to gradually build up and deliver something truly amazing.

Ok how does this work?

We have two ways for you to offer your support.

First, you can purchase our own crafted biltong packs, ensuring you get great value for your money. We've partnered with one of the best authentic biltong makers in Australia, and he will be our biltong supplier as we continue to grow our platform.

Secondly, you can support our cause by selecting one or two of our trial boxes. These boxes, filled with all Australian-made products, will be delivered directly to your door. We source from small producers to create a pack that will amaze you. But please understand that there will be a Limited Amount of Packs available and it will be a 4 Week turnaround or more depending on the producers.
